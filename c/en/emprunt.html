Borrow
All of our games at your disposal !
Members
Members can borrow our games for free during an entire week. To do so, find a committee member present at the entrance of our game nights, bring them the game that you want to borrow and show them your member card.

If you wish to borrow a game at any other time during the week, send a message on our Discord server, if a committee member is available, they will handle it with you.

By borrowing a game from Ludopoly, you are agreeing to our href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DReIX_h992ot8kpPvnGJSTemni2sW-59wlTye2LJiV8/edit?usp=sharing">lending terms.
Associations
As an association, you can borrow our games for free at one of your events or for your teambuildings.

To do so, send an e-mail to comite@ludopoly.ch and provide these information :
• The games you wish to borrow, we can also give you advice on which game to choose depending on the type of event and the attendees.
• The duration for which you need our games, meaning the approximate times for the start and the end of your event (if we have something planned at the same time where we need the requested games, we won’t be able to lend them).

Then, we set up a time and place to hand you the games and make you sign a lending contract, here is a copy of it.
