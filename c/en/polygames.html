PolyGames
Our big annual tournament !
How it works
Throughout the academic year, you can gather points by participating in our tournaments and try to reach the podium to receive great prizes ! The points are distributed as follows : 50 for first place, 32 for second place, 20 for third place and 4 points for every other competitor. You also obtain 4 points for each challenge completed during our game nights, each Thursday.

To simplify the points count, please always use the same e-mail address when registering to a tournament or upon the completion of a challenge. May the best win and good luck !
