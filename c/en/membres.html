Members
One card and many benefits !
Become a member
To become a member, you need to come to a game night and ask one of the committee members who are making the grilled cheeses at the entrance. For 5 francs you’ll obtain a membership card and will be able to enjoy all of our members’ benefits for the remainder of the semester.

You will have to show this card every time you want to use one of these benefits.
Borrow
By being a member, you can borrow our games. More information on the "Borrowing" page of this website.
Discount
By being a member, you also have access to a 10% discount on every purchase made at the Mighty Games stores.
Vote
Being a Ludopoly member also means having the opportunity to have a say on the commission’s future, by electing members of the presidency and committee but also by voting to pass internal rules changes. You can do all of this during our members’ assemblies (the commission’s governing body) which take place in May.
