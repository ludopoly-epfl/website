LUDOPOLY
Boardgaming at EPFL !
Presentation
Ludopoly is AGEPoly’s commission dedicated to boardgames ! With a year history and over 300 games, our goal is to share our passion for these games across the campus and give anyone the opportunity to spend a great time, outside the worries of everyday life.

That is why we host game nights each Thursday, which are free and open to everyone ! More than a hundred people show up weekly, so whether you come alone or with your friends, take any game you want, and enjoy !
