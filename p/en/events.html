Events
Every Thursday and Tuesday !
General
At Ludopoly, we are active each week of the semester, with game nights each Thursday and other events on Tuesdays, such as multiple tournaments (Poker, Catan, team games, …) or themed game nights. We also organize, at the start of the semester, an introduction to Magic The Gathering, followed by multiple drafts for the remainder of the semester. Longer events are also held on weekends, such as the LudoLan (a multi-game tournament spanning an entire weekend) or the LudoJam (a game creation contest).

And the best part is that all these events are free and open to everyone ! The only exception are our drafts, for which you need boosters, which can be bought at the event.
Game nights
Each Thursday, come find us in CM 1 120 (indicated on the map below) !

Everybody is welcome, anytime between 6 and 10 PM and is free to play any of our available games ! You can always ask a committee member if you have any questions, doubts or if you want some advice on which game to pick.

Last but not least, if you haven’t brought any food, no worries ! We sell croque-monsieurs (a type of grilled cheese) at the entrance of CM 1 120. We also offer multiple recipes to accommodate everyone’s diets.
